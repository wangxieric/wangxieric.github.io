Overview

The Interactive NLP Research Lab advances natural language processing techniques through principled interaction with four key pillars:

 Environment

Grounding language understanding in real-world contexts — from web documents and structured knowledge bases to dynamic, task-oriented environments where systems must reason and act.

 Human

Building conversational systems that understand user intent, model preferences, ask clarifying questions, and adapt over time — putting human needs at the centre of NLP research.

 Knowledge

Integrating structured and unstructured knowledge sources into language models — through retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs, and fact-grounded reasoning.

 Agents

Designing multi-agent NLP systems in which language models collaborate, critique, and specialise — enabling scalable, robust solutions for complex real-world problems.

Our work spans Conversational AI, Information Retrieval, Personalisation, and AI for Mental Health, and has been published at top venues including ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, SIGIR, AAAI, and CIKM.

Research Themes

  • Proactive Conversational Information Seeking — systems that ask targeted questions to resolve uncertainty and satisfy complex information needs
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) — neural IR systems that ground LLM outputs in retrieved evidence
  • User Simulation — synthetic users for scalable evaluation of dialogue and IR systems
  • Conversational Recommendation — preference elicitation and cold-start personalisation through dialogue
  • AI for Mental Health — LLM-based tools for depression screening and emotionally-aware conversation
